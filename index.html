Strategy & settings

These apply to every upload from now on. The metric itself is chosen each month when you upload, and is remembered.

Brokerage and STT
When on, both are deducted from cash on every buy, sell, short and cover, including trades you record by hand, so returns are shown after charges. Trades already made are not recalculated.

Borrow fee on short positions
A yearly rate charged on the market value of each short, counted by the day it is held. It is deducted from cash when you cover, add to a short, or apply a rebalance, and accrued fees are already counted in portfolio value.
With 50% and 50% the two books are the same size, so the portfolio starts dollar neutral. Each name on a side gets an equal share of that side's allocation.
Short sale proceeds are held as cash, and portfolio value is cash plus long positions minus short positions. A position missing from a month's sheet is never closed automatically: it is flagged so you can exit it yourself and record the real price.

Save to your account

Sign in, or create an account, and your portfolio is stored under it, so it is there on any browser or device and survives clearing site data. Only you can read it.
